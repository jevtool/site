Tools for Jev and every System One model

Test the judgment.

Jev answers a yes/no, choice or score question in about 300 ms for a fraction of a cent. jevtool tells you whether those answers are right for your data: accuracy, the cases it misses, a confidence cutoff that is safe to automate at, and what changes on the next Jev version.

Run an eval in your browser →Jev vs every alternative

npx jevtool eval example:support-triage

Open source, MIT, zero dependencies. Bring your own TypeSafe key.

HookPreToolUse
jev-guard
5 questions · ~0.4 s

Before Claude runs a command, writes a file or fetches a URL, Jev scores destructive, out-of-workspace, exfiltration, fit-to-task and severity. Blocks or asks. Never auto-approves, so your permission rules still apply.
